Case study · Aug 2026 — Present

Accesco Living

Designing grocery commerce for two very different users — the shopper finishing a checkout, and the delivery partner finishing a route.

Role
UI/UX Designer Intern
Platforms
Web & Mobile
Team
Led a group of design interns
Tools
Figma, prototyping, design systems

01 — Cart & checkout

Fewer decisions, faster checkout.

  1. 01CartReview items & quantities
  2. 02DeliveryAddress & slot
  3. 03PaymentClear totals, no surprises
  4. 04ConfirmationOrder status at a glance

I reimagined the end-to-end cart and checkout experience across web and mobile apps — focusing on clarity at each step and consistency between platforms, so customers moving between phone and desktop never have to relearn the flow.

02 — Delivery partners

Designed for one hand, bright sunlight and a ticking clock.

I crafted workflows and interfaces for delivery partners with a focus on speed, accessibility and efficient task execution in real-world delivery scenarios — large touch targets, high contrast, and the next action always obvious.

03 — Systems & leadership

Design system

Established cohesive components and interaction patterns for a consistent experience across multiple platforms.

Team leadership

Directed a cross-functional team of design interns — workload distribution, design reviews and UX mentorship.

Prototypes to production

Delivered high-fidelity prototypes and feature enhancements in close collaboration with stakeholders and engineering.
